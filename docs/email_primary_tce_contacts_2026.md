A QUICK NOTE ON BLUE SYNC

The auxiliary platform is separate from the Blue evaluation system.
Changes you make here reflect immediately on the TCE Admin website, but
they are pushed to Blue once per day during our nightly sync — typically
by the following morning. So if you add someone today at 2:00 PM, they
won't appear in Blue until tomorrow, but they'll be visible on the
auxiliary platform right away.

ROLES & ACCESS LEVELS — A REFRESHER

When adding or updating contacts, here's what each access flag means:

  **Level Type**
  ─────────────────
  • Subject Viewer — Receives launch notifications, reminders, and
    closing emails for TCE evaluations. This is the standard role for
    most departmental contacts.

  • Report Viewer — Receives only the automated email when TCE reports
    are released. Best for administrators who need access to results
    but don't need to manage the evaluation cycle.

  **Access Flags**
  ─────────────────
  • Static Report Access — Standard PDF TCE reports.

  • Dashboard Access — The new dynamic, interactive reports.
    **Important:** We have limited licenses for Dashboard Access. At
    this stage, we strongly recommend limiting it to:
      – 1 person per department
      – 2 people per college (maximum)

  • QB Access — Question Bank management. This is only relevant for
    colleges that are actively using the Question Bank feature. If your
    college isn't participating in the Question Bank, leave this off.

  **Multi-Department Contacts**
  ─────────────────
  A contact can be assigned to multiple departments. The system handles
  this cleanly — no need to create duplicate entries for someone who
  supports more than one unit.
