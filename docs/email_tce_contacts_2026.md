Subject: TCE Admin System — Updates for the New Academic Year

Dear TCE Contacts,

Greetings! I hope your summer is going well. As we gear up for the
upcoming academic year, I wanted to share several improvements we've
made to the TCE Admin System to make managing your course evaluations
smoother and more intuitive.

──────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT'S NEW

Verification Reports — the core of the system — have been refined to
give you a clearer picture of your evaluation coverage at a glance.
Each course now shows its TCE status with clear visual indicators:

  • Marked (green) — flagged for evaluation · good to go
  • Not Marked (grey) — not yet flagged · may need attention
  • Zero Enrollment (amber) — flagged but no students · likely exempt

You can filter by college, department, and term; search by course code,
title, or section number; and export your filtered view to CSV with one
click. Courses in bold have evaluation questions already assigned.

──────────────────────────────────────────

DATA FRESHNESS

Course data is pulled from SAP automatically every day at 3:00 AM, so
the information you see in the system stays up to date. SAP changes —
such as adding or removing an instructor from a course, correcting a
course title, or adjusting enrollment figures — cannot be made through
the TCE Admin website. Those changes must be made directly in SAP or
myUK if you have access; otherwise, contact your college registrar
contact. Please allow 12–24 hours for SAP changes to fully populate
across the TCE system.

──────────────────────────────────────────

GETTING AROUND

We've tried to make the system as user-friendly as possible. Here are
two built-in ways to get help:

  1. Interactive Walkthrough
     A guided tour launches automatically on your first sign-in. It
     walks you through the dashboard, navigation, verification reports,
     admin tools, and documentation. You can replay it anytime — just
     click your name in the top-right corner and select "Take the Tour."

  2. Full Documentation
     Detailed, role-specific guides are available at:
     https://facadv.uky.edu/tce-admin/documentation

     The documentation adapts to your role — Super Admin, College Admin,
     Department Admin, or Course Coordinator — and includes step-by-step
     instructions for verification reports, static reports, managing
     users, and working with the Question Bank (if you have access).
