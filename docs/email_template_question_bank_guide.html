TCE Question Bank

Guide for Departmental Contacts

Dear Departmental Contact,

We are pleased to introduce the TCE Question Bank, a tool that allows you to manage additional questions for your department's course evaluations. This guide will walk you through how to use the system and explain the new visual feedback features.

Getting Started

1 Access the Question Bank

Navigate to the Question Bank from your TCE Admin dashboard. You'll see a hierarchical tree view of your department's courses on the left side.

2 Select a Unit

Click on a Department, Course, or Section in the tree to view and manage its questions. Units with existing questions are shown in bold text.

3 View Questions

Once you select a unit, you'll see two tabs:

  • Course Questions - Questions about the course itself
  • Instructor Questions - Questions about the instructor

Managing Questions

Adding a Question

Click the "Add Question" button at the bottom of the questions panel. You can either:

  • Select an existing question from the Question Bank by searching
  • Create a new question by entering the question text and selecting the type (Multiple Choice or Open-Ended)

Editing a Question

Click the pencil icon on any question card to edit its text or change its type.

Removing a Question

Click the trash icon on any question card to remove it from the unit.

Important: As a departmental contact, all your changes require approval from a College Administrator before they take effect. You will see visual feedback on the page showing the status of your pending requests.

Understanding Visual Feedback

After you submit a change request, you'll see visual indicators in the Question Bank showing your pending changes. This helps you avoid submitting duplicate requests!

Pending Addition

Questions you've requested to add will appear with a green highlighted background:

How effective was the online discussion component of this course?

Awaiting approval - this question will be added once approved

Pending Removal

Questions you've requested to remove will appear with strikethrough text and a red-tinted background:

Pending Removal

How would you rate the course materials?

Awaiting approval - this question will be removed once approved

Pending Edit

Questions you've requested to edit will show both the current text (struck through) and your proposed new text (highlighted):

How effective was the instructor's teaching? → How effective was the instructor's use of active learning techniques?

Awaiting approval - changes shown above will be applied once approved

Viewing Your Pending Requests

To see all your pending requests in one place, click the "My Pending Requests" button in the top right corner of the Question Bank page. This page shows:

  • All your submitted change requests
  • The type of each change (Add, Edit, Remove)
  • When you submitted each request
  • The question text affected

Tips for Success

  • Check before submitting: Look for visual indicators to see if you've already requested a change
  • Be patient: Changes require College Administrator approval - you'll see them applied once approved
  • Use descriptive questions: Clear, specific questions yield better evaluation feedback
  • Review regularly: Check your "My Pending Requests" page to track your submissions
