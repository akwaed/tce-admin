TCE QUESTION BANK - Guide for Departmental Contacts
===============================================

Dear Departmental Contact,

We are pleased to introduce the TCE Question Bank, a tool that allows you
to manage additional questions for your department's course evaluations.
This guide will walk you through how to use the system and explain the
new visual feedback features.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

1. ACCESS THE QUESTION BANK
   Navigate to the Question Bank from your TCE Admin dashboard. You'll see
   a hierarchical tree view of your department's courses on the left side.

2. SELECT A UNIT
   Click on a Department, Course, or Section in the tree to view and manage
   its questions. Units with existing questions are shown in bold text.

3. VIEW QUESTIONS
   Once you select a unit, you'll see two tabs:
   - Course Questions: Questions about the course itself
   - Instructor Questions: Questions about the instructor


MANAGING QUESTIONS
------------------

ADDING A QUESTION
Click the "Add Question" button at the bottom of the questions panel.
You can either:
- Select an existing question from the Question Bank by searching
- Create a new question by entering the question text and selecting
  the type (Multiple Choice or Open-Ended)

EDITING A QUESTION
Click the pencil icon on any question card to edit its text or change
its type.

REMOVING A QUESTION
Click the trash icon on any question card to remove it from the unit.

*** IMPORTANT ***
As a departmental contact, all your changes require approval from a
College Administrator before they take effect. You will see visual
feedback on the page showing the status of your pending requests.


UNDERSTANDING VISUAL FEEDBACK
-----------------------------

After you submit a change request, you'll see visual indicators in the
Question Bank showing your pending changes. This helps you avoid
submitting duplicate requests!

VIEWING YOUR PENDING REQUESTS
-----------------------------

To see all your pending requests in one place, click the "My Pending
Requests" button in the top right corner of the Question Bank page.
This page shows:

- All your submitted change requests
- The type of each change (Add, Edit, Remove)
- When you submitted each request
- The question text affected


TIPS FOR SUCCESS
----------------

* Check before submitting: Look for visual indicators to see if you've
  already requested a change

* Be patient: Changes require College Administrator approval - you'll
  see them applied once approved

* Use descriptive questions: Clear, specific questions yield better
  evaluation feedback

* Review regularly: Check your "My Pending Requests" page to track
  your submissions


NEED HELP?

If you have questions or encounter any issues, please contact:
