A QUICK WALKTHROUGH

The Question Bank is organised hierarchically — College → Department →
Course → Section. All levels start collapsed so you can drill down to
exactly what you need.

  Finding questions:
  1. Go to Question Bank → Browse.
  2. Click your college name to expand it and see your departments.
  3. Click a department to expand it and see its courses.
  4. Click a course to see its sections (e.g., CS215-001, CS215-002).
  5. Click a section to see its assigned questions.

Each course has two tabs:
  • Instructor Questions — evaluation questions about the instructor.
  • Course Questions — evaluation questions about the course itself.

  Adding a question:
  Click Add Question at the bottom of the question panel. Choose the
  type: Selection (multiple choice — you provide the answer options) or
  Comment (open-ended text response). Fill in the text and save.

  Editing a question:
  Click the pencil icon on any question card to change its text or type.

  Removing a question:
  Click the trash icon on any question to remove it.

Use the Term dropdown at the top to switch between academic terms.

──────────────────────────────────────────

IMPORTANT: YOUR ROLE AS PRIMARY CONTACT

As a primary TCE contact, you have two powers that regular department
contacts do not:

  1. You can add and modify questions yourself — but only if the
     **QB flag** is checked in your own profile. To verify or enable
     this, go to Admin List, find your own record, and confirm the QB
     checkbox is ticked under Access Settings. If it isn't, turn it on
     and save.

  2. You have **final approval authority** over every question change
     submitted by anyone in your college. Department-level contacts can
     submit additions, edits, and deletions, but those changes sit in a
     Pending state until you review and either approve or reject them.
     You'll receive a notification when submissions are waiting for you —
     check Question Bank → Pending regularly.

──────────────────────────────────────────

GRANTING QB-ONLY ACCESS TO OTHERS

If you have department contacts who need to work with questions but
should **not** have access to your static reports or the dashboard, you
can grant them QB-only access. Here's how:

  • Go to Admin List → Add Administrator (or edit an existing contact).
  • Under Access Settings, check **only** the QB (Question Bank) box.
  • Leave Static Report Access **unchecked**.
  • Leave Dashboard Access **unchecked**.
  • Save the record.

That contact will see the Question Bank tab in their menu and can submit
changes for your approval — but they won't be able to view static
reports, verification data, or the dashboard.

──────────────────────────────────────────

VISUAL FEEDBACK ON PENDING CHANGES

When you or your department contacts submit changes, the Question Bank
shows clear visual indicators so you never accidentally submit duplicate
requests:

  • Pending Addition — green background with "Pending Addition" badge.
  • Pending Removal — red-tinted background with strikethrough text.
  • Pending Edit — old text struck through, new text highlighted.

Department contacts can also track all their submissions by clicking the
"My Pending Requests" button in the top-right corner of the Question
Bank page.
